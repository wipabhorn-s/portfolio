Windows apps for quality control on a production line: checking scanned parts, weighing cartons and printing labels.

Barcode Inspection

Checks every scanned part before it goes into a carton: barcode format, duplicates, check digits and the results of earlier stations, then records PASS or FAIL in PostgreSQL.

  • VB.NET
  • WinForms
  • PostgreSQL
  • MSTest

Carton Weight Inspection

Weighs each carton at the end of the packing line on a serial-port scale, checks it against the product's limits, signals pass or fail through an Arduino and prints the carton label.

  • VB.NET
  • WinForms
  • Serial ports
  • Arduino
